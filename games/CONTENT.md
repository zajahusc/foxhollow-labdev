The brief targeted 100 constructed puzzles per game (20 per level). This release is **60 short for The Fourth Thing and 65 short for The Odd Rule**. It deliberately uses a smaller checked bank. The 242 frozen numerical daily instances (121 per game) and unlimited generated practice are additional instances of existing families, not a substitute claim of 200 distinct authored puzzles or 317 independent rule families.

Each game uses 25 spelling/structural families and 10 numerical families. Fourth Thing adds five constrained sequence puzzles. Families recur across the two distinct mechanics. There is no specialist trivia or unapproved semantic/cultural content. Difficulty is estimated from property familiarity and number of reasoning steps, not player telemetry.

## Verification scope

All published clues and options are checked by exact predicates. Fourth Thing has three distinct clues and four options with exactly one predicate match, or an explicitly specified numeric progression with a fixed fourth value. Odd Rule has exactly five matches and one violation, with rule-description distractors that fail for at least one matching item. Every item has a predicate-generated explanation. All have three progressive hints. Names and definitions are recorded before play.

The alternative-answer scan compares all 35 published rule predicates in the relevant domain (written words or numbers). It rejects a Fourth Thing completion pointing to a different choice or an Odd Rule outsider pointing to a different card. This is a bounded ambiguity screen, not mathematical proof against all possible linguistic interpretations. No LLM or fictitious human approval is used. No semantic candidate is promoted as editorially approved.

Spelling conventions are explicit: A/E/I/O/U are vowels, Y is a consonant; letter order means English alphabet order; the keyboard rule specifies US QWERTY. Roman-letter puzzles test membership in IVXLCDM, not valid Roman-numeral grammar. Numeric definitions use exact integer operations. Free responses use normalization and exact approved aliases; no fuzzy semantic grading is used.

## Scheduling

The explicit schedule covers October 21, 2026 through October 20, 2027 inclusive. Two of every three dates use the constructed bank; the third uses a unique frozen generated instance. Constructed puzzles recur after their cycle, in deterministic order. All historical assignments and generated daily objects are checked in. Adding rules or practice generation does not reassign past days.
