How to play

You get one valid English word to guess today’s hidden word. The word length is your starting clue. Green means the right letter in the right place; yellow means the letter belongs elsewhere; gray means no remaining match.

Earn 2 points per exact letter and 1 per misplaced letter. Guess the whole word for a bonus of 25 × its length. Letters bought as hints earn no exact-letter points.

Spend earned points to reveal a position: 25 for the first letter, 50 for the second, 150 for the third. Tap a blank tile after choosing Buy a letter. Purple marks bought letters. Purchases are final and saved immediately.

A new word arrives at midnight New York time. Progress and points stay in this browser; they do not sync with the iOS app. No account or purchases needed.
